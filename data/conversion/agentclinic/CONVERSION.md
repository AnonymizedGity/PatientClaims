# AgentClinic-MedQA Extended → common case schema

Target format: [case_schema.md](../case_schema.md). Read it fully first.
Worked example: `bundles/agentclinic-medqa-ext-001.json`, built from `source/agentclinic-medqa-ext-001.json`.

These are synthetic OSCE cases generated by GPT-4 from MedQA questions. Each
source file (`source/<case>.json`, built deterministically from the raw data) has:

- `demographics_text`, `chief_complaint` (cc, the `Primary_Symptom`)
- `fragments`: patient text pieces with fixed ids `…-pNNN` (History, each
  Secondary_Symptom, Past_Medical_History, Social_History, Review_of_Systems,
  and in 9 cases medication or family-history fields)
- `exam_leaves` (`…-eNNN`) and `test_leaves` (`…-iNNN`): one objective value per source path
- `correct_diagnosis`; `objective_for_doctor` (the task line shown to the doctor in AgentClinic)
- `pool_flags.visible_gold_alias_partitions`: where a screen of the pool found the
  diagnosis name in visible text (21 cases)

Output: `bundles/<case>.json` = `{"patient", "environment", "gold", "qc"}`. Both must print OK:

```
python -I data/conversion/validate_cases.py --bundles data/conversion/agentclinic/bundles <case>
python -I data/conversion/agentclinic/check_coverage.py <case>
```

Treat every warning as a question to answer. Fix it, or justify it in `qc.notes`.

## Patient facts

- **One fact = one answerable statement**, in the patient's own first-person
  words, keeping every qualifier (durations, frequencies, "mild", sides, numbers).
- **Deduplicate.** The source repeats things: the same symptom appears in History,
  Secondary_Symptoms and the chief complaint. Make one fact citing all
  of its sources. Two facts must never say the same thing.
- **`item`** names a *finding* that can be present or absent, with no answer in it
  (`dark urine`, `abdominal pain: location`). A normal state is the finding
  **absent** (`loss of appetite` absent for "my appetite is fine"). Never use
  a neutral label with a normal answer marked present.
- **Polarity by meaning**: "I have no appetite" is present; never turn a
  missing answer into absent.
- **Attributes are children** (`parent` + `attribute`). A child has its
  parent's `category` and `system`. If one attribute applies to several
  symptoms ("all of this started a month ago", "worse after activity"), make
  it a standalone fact with a general item (see the example).
- **Patient voice.** Replace clinical terms with the patient's words
  ("diplopia" → seeing double, "dysuria" → it burns when I pee); clinical
  terms go in `aliases`. Signs that only an observer can report
  ("patient appears tired", "disorganized speech", "psychomotor agitation") are
  not patient facts. They belong to the exam: if the exam leaves already contain
  them, cite nothing new; otherwise put them in `qc.extras.acting_notes`. For
  psychiatric cases, write what the patient experiences or believes ("I think
  the NSA is spying on me"), not the diagnostic label ("delusions of persecution").
- **Collateral history** ("his wife adds that…") becomes what the patient was told:
  "My wife says I've been more withdrawn for the last 3 months."
- **Meta phrases.** A phrase that states a negative is a denial, including the
  clinical shorthand "No X reported", "None reported", "did not report X" and
  "No significant past medical history (provided)" (one `chronic medical problems`
  fact). A phrase that only says the information is missing ("not mentioned",
  "no mention of", "not specified", "information not provided", "no relevant social
  history provided") is not a denial: list it in `qc.excluded_sources` and leave the
  topic unknown. Never derive specific denials (no alcohol, no drugs) from a broad
  meta phrase.
- **Blanket denials** ("no other symptoms", "otherwise healthy") use the schema's
  standard items: `other symptoms`, `other <scope>`, `chronic medical problems`,
  `other medical problems`.
- **Facts only in `demographics_text`** (gestational age, parity, home state) cite
  `"demographics_text"` in `qc.fact_sources`.
- **Medication lists**: one `medication` fact per drug. Contraception follows
  the schema: hormonal or device methods are `medication`; condoms, withdrawal
  and a partner's vasectomy are `habit_exposure`.
- **`category`** from the schema's six values. **`system`** is required on every
  `symptom` fact. **`time`** is relative to this visit. Scores get `value` + `scale`.
- **`opening`**: the fact(s) of the chief complaint. If `Primary_Symptom` names
  two complaints, list both facts. If there is no chief complaint (case 132), use
  the visit reason from the History.
- **Diagnosis names in patient facts** only when the patient was told (a prior
  diagnosis). Otherwise rephrase as experience. Any gold-diagnosis word left in
  a fact must be listed in `qc.leakage` with the reason.

## Environment

- **Vital signs**: one `vital` record per parameter. AgentClinic vitals are part of
  the exam (not shown at the door), so no `initial`. A value like "Normal" stays
  as "Normal".
- **Exams**: one record per thing a doctor would ask to examine. Combine
  Inspection / Auscultation / Percussion / Palpation of one region into one
  record ("abdominal examination": "Inspection: …; auscultation: …; …"). Keep
  neurological components separate (cranial nerves, motor strength, reflexes,
  sensation, coordination, gait, mental status). Named special tests (Murphy,
  McBurney, Homans, Romberg, Babinski, straight-leg raise, …) each get their
  own record. Leaves with no information ("N/A", "Not performed") go in
  `qc.excluded_exam_lines` with a reason.
- **Tests**: one record per test a doctor would order.
  - Group a standard panel when two or more of its members are present:
    complete blood count, basic or comprehensive metabolic panel / electrolytes,
    liver function tests, coagulation, lipid panel, thyroid function, urinalysis,
    cardiac enzymes, arterial blood gas, viral hepatitis serology.
  - The `result` lists each member as "name value".
  - The member names go into `aliases` ("hemoglobin", "AST").
  - Otherwise one record per test. Imaging is one record per study; pathology one
    per specimen; ECG, EEG, EMG one each.
- **`name`** must not reveal the diagnosis ("Skin_Biopsy_of_Psoriatic_Patch" →
  "skin biopsy of elbow lesion"). A `result` may state what the test shows
  ("findings consistent with psoriasis"), because that is the test's answer.
  When a result names the gold diagnosis, list it in `qc.leakage`.
- **`result`**: exactly what the doctor is told, in plain clinical English, with no
  review commentary (that goes in `qc.finding_flags`). Bedside examinations return
  the finding without the interpretation ("Positive: right-sided nystagmus", not
  "…, suggestive of BPPV"); written reports keep their conclusions.

## Harmonisation

After all batches pass, `scan.py` lists cross-case inconsistencies and
`harmonize.py` applies the reviewed edits (blanket-denial labels, sleep and breast
systems, bedside interpretations, missing everyday gold names). Then
`build_release.py` writes `release/`.

## Gold

- `diagnosis`: the source `correct_diagnosis`; only fix capitalisation and obvious typos.
- `aliases`: per the schema: synonyms, abbreviations, lay names, and more
  specific forms entirely within the diagnosis; never broader terms.
- No `differential` (AgentClinic has none).

## QC

- `fact_sources`: fragment ids (full form) or `cc` for every fact.
- `quotes`: shortest exact supporting fragment of the source text, for facts
  with numbers, qualifiers, or wording that could be disputed.
- `flags`, `finding_flags`: doubts, keyed by fact or finding id.
- `finding_sources`: `{source: <source path(s) joined with " + ">, source_text, exam_ids}` per finding.
- `leakage`: `[{"fact_id" or "id": …, "reason": …}]`, or `[]`.
- `source_issues`: contradictions, typos, implausible values, diagnosis text in visible fields.
- `notes`: decisions a reviewer should know.
- `extras`: `objective_for_doctor` (always), and `acting_notes` when you moved observer-only behaviour there.
- `excluded_sources`, `excluded_exam_lines`: `[{id, reason}]`.
