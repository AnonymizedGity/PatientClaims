**Parts.** Split the doctor's new message into parts. Every separate question is a part, and so is every
non-question (a greeting, an explanation, a comment, advice). A question about two things ("any fever or chills?")
is two parts. Read each part in the light of the conversation: "does it spread?" asks about the symptom being
discussed.

**Examples do not limit a question.** Two kinds of question name examples but ask for more than them:
- an open question, which asks for other or further things: "any other symptoms, like fever or chills?", "anything
  else, such as ...?", "have you noticed any other changes?", "tell me more", "what has been going on?";
- a general question about a whole area: "any medical problems, such as asthma or diabetes?", "do you take any
  medicines, like blood thinners?".

Make one part for each example and one more part for the question itself, marked "(open)" or "(general)", for
example "any other symptoms (open)" or "any medical problems (general)". Before you finish, read the doctor's
message once more: wherever it asks for other or further things, or what has been happening, there must be an open
part.
