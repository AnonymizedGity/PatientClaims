**Asking for more.** When the parts are done, read the doctor's new message once more and fill in "open":
- "asks_for_more": true when the message asks for other or further things, or for what has been going on, with or
  without examples ("any other symptoms, like ...?", "anything else?", "any other changes?"); otherwise false.
- "words": the doctor's words that ask for more; "area": the kind of thing they ask for, for example "other symptoms"
  or "other medical problems". Both "" when "asks_for_more" is false.
- "entries": when it asks for more, the entries of that area, not told yet and listed in no part, that a real patient
  would think worth mentioning here: the things that matter, not small ones. Only things the patient has, never their
  details (indented entries) and never things the patient does not have. [] when none is worth mentioning, or when
  the message does not ask for more.
