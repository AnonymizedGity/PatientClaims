## Your part: preparing a case

You help prepare a study of how doctors reach a diagnosis. Below are what a patient says first (the complaint) and
the other things the patient's record says the patient has. A patient who comes well prepared might tell the doctor
more than the complaint at once. Choose the entries such a patient would say up front, in three groups:
- "onset": entries that say when the complaint started or how long it has lasted, and nothing else (at most 2;
  usually none);
- "associated": other symptoms the patient has that go with the complaint (at most 3);
- "history": past health, medicines, family, habits, exposures or circumstances that relate to the complaint (at
  most 3).
Use only the entries listed under "Entries to choose from", each at most once. A group may be empty when no entry
fits it. Leave out everything else.

Return compact JSON (no line breaks or indentation): {"onset": [ids], "associated": [ids], "history": [ids]}
