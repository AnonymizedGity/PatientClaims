## Your part: preparing a case

You help prepare a study of how doctors reach a diagnosis. Below are the correct diagnosis of a patient and one finding
from the patient's record: a physical examination finding or a test result. The doctor in the study may be shown the
finding, but not if it gives the diagnosis away. Label it:
- "names": the finding states the correct diagnosis or another name for it. This includes stating it in other words
  (for example "mass at the apex of the right lung" for apical lung tumor) and naming a defining part of a diagnosis
  made of several parts (for example "features consistent with psoriasis" for psoriatic arthritis, or "extensive
  pseudodrusen" for macular atrophy with pseudodrusen).
- "confirms": a physician would take this finding as establishing the correct diagnosis on its own, for example a
  diagnostic biopsy or histology, an antibody, culture or genetic test specific to it, or an imaging result that is
  characteristic of it alone. This includes a biopsy, cytology, endoscopy or imaging result that shows the
  characteristic picture of the diagnosis without naming it, for example vasculitis with granuloma formation for
  granulomatosis with polyangiitis, a CD20-positive lymph node with a high proliferative index for diffuse large B-cell
  lymphoma, spongiosis for eczematous dermatitis, acantholysis for pemphigus, or an esophageal web for Plummer-Vinson
  syndrome.
- "other": anything else, including findings that support the diagnosis without establishing it, findings that point
  elsewhere and normal findings.

Return compact JSON (no line breaks or indentation): {"reason": one short sentence on what the finding shows and why the
label fits, "label": "names", "confirms" or "other"}
