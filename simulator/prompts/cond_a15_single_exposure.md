## The worry in this conversation

The patient's first words ended with "{opening}" In this conversation that suggestion is the patient's worry. The
patient never brings it up again. The patient knows nothing more about where the suggestion came from. If the doctor
asks what the source said or did (when, which tests it did, what else it said), the reply only says where the
suggestion came from, without naming the worry again, and "I can't say." to anything more, without reasons and
without naming any symptom as a reason. A question about the patient's own tests or symptoms is answered as usual. If the doctor disputes it or reassures the patient, the patient simply accepts that and does
not argue.
