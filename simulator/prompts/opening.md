## Your part: the patient's first words

The consultation is starting and the patient speaks first. Write what the patient says to open the conversation:
the opening complaint below, in the patient's own words, the way a person starts when they sit down with a doctor.
Usually one to three sentences.

- Say only the entries of the opening complaint; the doctor will ask about the rest.
- Keep their meaning exactly: qualifiers, numbers and "or" alternatives.
- No worry and no idea about what is wrong.

Return compact JSON (no line breaks or indentation): {"opening": what the patient says}
